## Scope

The whole single-page portfolio (`src/App.tsx` and its sections). Visitor mode: Persuade. A hiring manager should leave having opened a live project and holding the resume or a contact route.

## Constraints

Redesign of the cream + italic-serif look, chosen by the user after a slop audit. Keep every fact, project, link, the resume PDF and the playlist. Body text 16px or larger, text contrast 4.5:1 or better, keyboard reachable, reduced motion honoured.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The design is the starch resist, the build is the dye, and the pattern comes through intact. Refuses the cream ground, italic serif display and tracked-mono eyebrow labels of the default portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Adire eleko cloth. Deep indigo ground (#141B3D) with mid-indigo panels, undyed cool cloth white (#EEF0F5) as the second field, hand-drawn resist line motifs in white on indigo panels, one strict square panel grid. One warm thread only: turmeric (#F2B437) marks primary actions and nothing else. Wide sharp grotesk display (Archivo, expanded), a hyperlegible body face, ruled ledgers instead of chips.

STORY: The visitor sees in one line that Demi designs and builds the same interface, opens Weatherly live, scans the stack in a ledger, then downloads the resume or emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Indigo field. Left seven columns: the headline about designing then building, a one-line role and Lagos sentence, See the work (turmeric) and Download resume (outline). Right five columns: the mirrored adire field, one hand-drawn tile flipped across an 8 by 8 grid so edge shapes close into rings, diamonds and leaves (the owner chose this over the cell grid). Below lg it becomes an 8 by 4 strip under the actions. Nav on the indigo above, hairline rule beneath.

FORM: Adire Cloth, position 4 on the grounded list, seed:0d3b4107bdc04044. Raises: one warm thread only (from Nixie Counter); one strict square panel grid governs every section (from ASCII Scene); screenshots shown whole and named beneath (from Lotería Night); project facts ruled into a ledger (from Forge Scale). Signature interaction: the motif panels act as section markers and draw their resist lines when a section enters, once, skipped under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
